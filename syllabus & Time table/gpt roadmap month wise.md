## 5. DAILY 6–8 HOUR TIMETABLE##

I recommend targeting about 7 productive hours, rather than trying to force 8 hours every day.

Daily Schedule
Time	Activity
8:30–9:00	Revision / planning
9:00–11:00	Java + DSA lecture + notes
11:00–11:30	Break
11:30–1:00	DSA coding practice
1:00–2:00	Lunch/rest
2:00–3:30	Full Stack learning
3:30–4:00	Break
4:00–5:30	Full Stack coding/project
5:30–6:00	Break
6:00–7:00	AI / CS / Interview preparation
7:00–7:30	Revision + GitHub/notes

This gives roughly 7 hours of serious study.

## 6. WEEKLY SYSTEM ##

Use this pattern every week:

Monday–Friday

DSA + Development + AI/Interview

Saturday

Project + DSA revision + pending lectures

Sunday

Weekly revision + mock test + backlog clearing

Don't allow backlog to become larger than one week.

7. YOUR DAILY PRIORITY ORDER

When you have limited time, follow this order:

1. DSA

2. Development

3. AI

4. Project

5. Interview theory

6. Aptitude

Do not spend 2 hours making beautiful notes while doing only 20 minutes of coding.

Your ratio should be approximately:

30% learning + 50% coding/project work + 20% revision/interview
